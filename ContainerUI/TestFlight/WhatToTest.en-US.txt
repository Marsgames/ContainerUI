---------- 0.2 ----------
New:
- Tap any container row to open its detail sheet (resources, environment, mounts, and a Logs tab).
- Delete a container or image from the list — a confirmation dialog appears before removal.
- Pull an image using the download button in the Images toolbar; enter any OCI reference.
- Add and remove container registries (Docker Hub, GHCR, etc.) with login credentials stored securely in the Keychain.

---------- 0.1 ----------
New:
- Containers are now listed with their status, image, and action buttons (start, stop, restart).
- Images are listed with their repository and tag.
- The app detects whether Apple's container runtime is installed and running, and guides you through fixing it if not.
- The app remembers its window size and the last selected section between launches.

Improved:
- Action buttons show a spinner on their row while an operation is in progress.
- A warning appears when a container exits immediately after being started (e.g. no TTY).

Under the hood:
- Technical and reliability improvements.
